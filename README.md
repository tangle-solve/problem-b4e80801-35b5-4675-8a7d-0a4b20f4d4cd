### CLIM-000120: Fifteen-year superhot geothermal wells
- **Subfield:** Low-carbon electricity generation
- **Open:** Develop wells, seals, sensors, and fluid systems operating above 375°C and 22 MPa for at least fifteen years despite cycling and corrosion.
- **Status:** Superhot resources have been accessed experimentally; ARPA-E’s 2025 SUPERHOT program targets sustained-production durability.
